tsrs-derived strategies in typescript-go-tsrs
Copyright 2026 Max Schwenk and the tsrs contributors

This fork translates strategies from tsrs, a Rust port of the TypeScript Go
compiler, into Go. The translated implementations are modified for Go's
collector, type layouts, caches, and concurrency rules. Source references and
translation decisions are recorded in docs/backports.
